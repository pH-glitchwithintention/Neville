Write 3–5 statements that declare your dream is already real. Start each with "I AM" or "I have" or "I feel." Write them as truth, not as wishes.

💬 What Will You Say?

Write 2–3 things you will actually say out loud — to yourself, to others — as if your dream is already happening.
